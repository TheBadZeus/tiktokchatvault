## What this app is
TikTok Chat Vault is an independent app that stores copies of direct messages from your own TikTok account on your own
device. It is not affiliated with TikTok.

## What it collects
Only what you explicitly import or switch on:
- Message text, sender names/IDs, conversation names, timestamps, attachment references and attachment files you import.
- Technical records of the app's own activity (when something was imported, verified, exported or deleted). These
  never contain message text.
- App settings such as your theme choice.

The app never collects your TikTok password, session cookies or access tokens.

## Where it is stored
Only on this device, in the app's private storage. [After Milestone 5: the message database is encrypted with a key
protected by the Android Keystore.] Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app.

## Sharing
The app does not upload messages anywhere and contains no analytics or advertising. Data leaves the device only when
you export it and choose where to save it.

## Your controls
- Turn off notification capture at any time in Android's notification-access settings.
- Delete all archived data from Settings → Data deletion. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
- Exports you save elsewhere are no longer controlled by the app; protect or delete them yourself.

## Other people's information
Messages include other people's words and usernames. Use the archive only for legitimate personal purposes and in line
with the law where you live.

## Evidence
Integrity hashes help detect accidental changes to the app's own records. They do not prove a message is authentic and
do not guarantee a record will be accepted as evidence.
